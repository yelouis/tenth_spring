# Quest Roster — the 110 City Quests and 60 Park Quests

This is **starting content** for the side quests promised in `design_interiors_and_quests.md` §5.5. It was written in the pass-19 content review (2026-10-21) and extended in pass 20 (2026-10-22: the Glow, and 24 new parks for Decision 32), at the human's request ("Review the landmarks, puzzles, background stories that were created and see if you can make them better"). Like the spawn tables, it is tunable: the human may swap or rewrite any row.

**Rules for every quest:**
- **Original text only** (`design_story_and_world.md` §9). Real public places keep their real names. Pokémon are named only for readability; data uses dex numbers.
- **Every quest reveals one piece of what happened** — the last column. Read together, a city's quests tell how it went dark, in the voice of its region's rule (`design_story_and_world.md` §3.1).
- **Rewards are written generically** ("a TM", "a rare ball"). Item and move numbers are chosen when the quest data is written (`game/data/quests/`, Phase 7).
- **Quests are play state.** The sync ingest never writes them (golden invariant 1).
- **Not counted here:** the 9 Truth quests, 7 key-item quests, and 78 legendary hint quests (`design_interiors_and_quests.md` §5.2–5.4), and the five cross-country chains.

**Kinds** are from `design_interiors_and_quests.md` §3 (puzzles) and §5.4 (quest patterns).
